Make a sale

A short checkout guide for the Windows till and mobile app.

Windows till

  1. Open New Sale. Scan the barcode or search and select the product.
  2. Check the basket. Confirm quantity, selling price, discounts and total. If permitted, Edit changes a price for this sale; check the updated total.
  3. Select Pay. Choose cash, card or the supported split-payment option.
  4. Confirm the money received. For cash, enter the amount received and check change. For card, complete payment on the separate terminal before confirming it in Posnic.
  5. Complete once. Wait for the successful receipt/result. Print the receipt and check the sale in history.

If the payment result is uncertain, check the terminal and sales history before retrying. A printer error is not a reason to take payment again.

Basket Review extension

Review the customer’s basket before completing payment. For mixed cash/card, enter the cash portion, check the remaining card amount, take that amount on the terminal and confirm the terminal result.

Pay → Print ticket prints an unpaid basket. It does not record payment or reduce stock. Return to the sale to take payment. For a completed sale, use the saved receipt’s reprint action.

Mobile checkout

  1. Open Sell, scan or search for items and review the current sale.
  2. Select Charge, check the amount and choose the payment method.
  3. Confirm only after receiving payment. Check Receipts and any pending-sync status.

Keep the app connected until pending sales finish syncing. Do not repeat a pending sale on another device.

Receipt printing

In the Windows app, open Settings → Printers & paper, choose the real printer and save settings on that till.

MethodWhen to use it
Exact designPreserves receipt graphics and alignment.
Fast receiptText-oriented ESC/POS output. Graphics or unsupported content may fall back to Exact.
Windows driverUses the installed printer driver.

Web printing uses the browser print dialog. Select the actual printer rather than Microsoft Print to PDF. Drawer operation needs the configured desktop hardware connection.
