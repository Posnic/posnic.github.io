Check, verify and update stock

Use the correct shop and branch. A count is the quantity actually on the shelf, not the quantity to add.

Find and verify stock first

  1. On the till, open Inventory → Items. On the phone, open Inventory → Find stock.
  2. Scan the barcode or search for the product. Check its name, pack/unit and On hand quantity.
  3. Count the physical units. Recheck any unexpected difference before changing the system.

Example: the system shows 10 and you count 8. Enter 8 as the counted quantity. For a delivery of 5 new units, use Receive stock and enter 5 instead.

Update from the Windows till

  1. Open Inventory → Stock Counts → Start recount.
  2. Select the items, enter the actual counted quantities and a useful reason/reference.
  3. Review the proposed stock changes, then confirm/apply them using an account with stock permissions.
  4. Check Inventory logs and reopen the item to verify the resulting quantity.

Bulk stock update is available on Stock Counts for larger changes. Review the proposed values before applying. A saved draft worksheet alone does not update stock.

Update from the phone

  1. Open Inventory and choose Count stock, Receive stock, Damage or Loss.
  2. Add or scan items, enter quantities and a reason/reference. Use at most 200 items per operation.
  3. Choose Submit for review. Review the items, quantities and reason.
  4. An authorised user chooses Approve / Confirm stock changes. A server connection is required.
  5. Wait for Complete, then verify Stock history and the item’s current on-hand quantity.

Understand the status

StatusWhat to do
Draft / PreparedSaved for review; this is not confirmation that stock changed.
ApplyingWait and check server status.
CompleteVerify the new quantity and history.
ConflictRefresh and recount unresolved items. Already-applied lines stay applied.

If the connection drops or the result is uncertain, keep the draft and check server status before retrying. Other tills show updates after successful synchronisation; refresh and check their connection if quantities differ.
