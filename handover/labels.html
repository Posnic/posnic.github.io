Shelf and product labels

The same label can be used on a shelf or as a product sticker when your paper and printer support it.

Choose products and copies

  1. Open Inventory → Items and choose Shelf / product label printing from the available item printing action.
  2. Click a product row or checkbox to select it. Select one or several products.
  3. Set copies beside each product. One copy prints once; a higher number repeats that product.

An A4 sheet can contain different products, repeated copies of the same product, or a mixture.

Choose paper and design

  1. Choose Label roll or A4 sheet, then a matching predefined size or custom dimensions.
  2. For A4, check rows/columns, sheet margins, gaps and the starting position. Match pre-cut sticker stock exactly.
  3. Adjust product name, price, shop name and additional text sizes independently. Watch the live preview.
  4. Choose Barcode, QR code or None. A missing barcode does not prevent the remaining details printing. QR can use the product code or your custom content.
  5. Enable Image if wanted. Use the saved product photo on the left or right and adjust its width. Colour output needs a colour printer; thermal output is monochrome.

Keep the complete code and its white border visible. Test scanning after changing size.

Print one test, then the batch

  1. Check the preview and the products/copies summary on the Print step.
  2. Choose the correct printer and method in the Windows app. In a web browser, use its print dialog.
  3. For browser printing, use matching paper dimensions, actual size / 100%, no browser margins and no headers/footers. A 297 mm paper length can feed unnecessary blank paper for a short roll label.
  4. Print one test label. Check orientation, text, barcode/QR scanning and physical edges before printing the batch.

Direct thermal mode offers cut after each label, cut after the batch or no cut on a supported cutter. With driver or web printing, configure cutting in the printer driver. The printer’s head-to-cutter distance may leave a top gap.
